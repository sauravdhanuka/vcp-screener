{# MR Visual Checklist — human reference, not computed #}

MR Checklist

{# Green Flags #}

Green Flags

  • ● 200-SMA intact (price above or recently above)
  • ● Clean prior support visible
  • ● RSI divergence (lower price low, higher RSI low)
  • ● Volume exhaustion (spike then dry-up)
  • ● Sector holding up
  • ● Reversal candle closed (hammer / engulfing)
  • ● Most negative z-score among candidates
{# Red Flags #}

Red Flags

  • ● Below declining 200-SMA (Stage 4)
  • ● Fundamental catalyst (earnings miss, debt crisis)
  • ● Walking the lower Bollinger Band
  • ● No volume climax (slow bleed, no capitulation)
  • ● Entire sector collapsing
  • ● ADX > 30 and rising (strong trend)
  • ● Near earnings or major event
{# 30-Second Visual Scan #}

30-Second Scan

  1. Zoom out to 1-year daily. Overall trend up or flat? If no, skip.
  2. Price visibly stretched below MAs? If no, skip.
  3. Reversal candle or RSI divergence forming? If no, deprioritize.
  4. Volume dried up after a spike? If yes, top of list.
